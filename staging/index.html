About QuizPrep

QuizPrep helps you get ready for your quizzes by practicing the way that actually builds understanding — one learning objective at a time, with questions that adjust to how you’re doing. Answer well and the questions step up from recalling facts to applying them; miss one and you get a hint and a pointer to exactly what to re-read. There’s nothing to install and no account to create.

You practice, see where you stand, and upload your session to Canvas when you’re done. Your progress is saved right here in your browser, and only the file you choose to upload ever leaves your device. It’s a study tool — a place to find and close the gaps before they count.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account, or to install anything?
No. Open the link from your instructor and start — everything runs in your browser.
Do I have to enter my name?
Once, so your instructor can match your practice to you. If your class uses anonymous mode, the app uses a random code from this device instead, and your instructor still identifies you through Canvas.
Who can see my answers?
By default, only you — until you upload a session file to Canvas. Nothing is sent anywhere automatically, and no server stores your work.
Why do the questions get harder or easier?
Each objective has a ladder: recall, then understanding, then applying it to a new situation. First-try correct answers move you up; a miss steps you back down, so you spend your time where you need it.
What happens when I get one wrong?
You get a nudge toward your mistake and a chance to try again. If you’re still stuck, you’ll see the exact chapter section to re-read (often with a short recap), and sometimes an easier follow-up to rebuild.
What does “mastered” mean?
You’ve mastered an objective when you answer correctly on the first try several times in a row at its hardest available level — two in a row for an application-level objective, three for one that tops out at understanding. Mastery sticks across sessions on this device.
How long is a session?
As long or short as you choose — a set number of questions, or “keep going until I master these objectives.” The app estimates the length and caps it so it never drags on.
Can I stop and come back?
Yes. Your progress saves automatically, and an unfinished session picks up right where you left off when you reopen the link.
How do I turn my practice in?
Download your session file at the end and upload it to the Canvas assignment your instructor set up. You can re-download any past session from your progress report if you forget.
Why is a chapter greyed out?
Your instructor releases chapters on a schedule; a locked chapter shows the date it opens.
Can I keep a bad practice run from counting?
Yes. In your progress report, untick “counts toward mastery” on any session. It stays in your history, but its results no longer affect which objectives show as mastered.
Will I lose my progress?
Only if you clear this site’s browser data, or switch to a different device or browser — progress is saved per device.
There’s a “refresh” bar at the top — what is it?
A newer version is available; tap it to update. Nothing you’ve done is lost.
Is this graded? Are the answers hidden securely?
It’s practice. What you turn in shows that you practiced — it isn’t a secured exam, so treat it as a study tool, not a test.
A question looks wrong to me — what do I do?
Tell your instructor. They can review it and, if needed, pass it along to be fixed.
