Automated checks

Structural checks (errors must be zero to ship) plus soft quality warnings — a faithful port of tools/validate_bank.py, including the length-tell and self-defining-option flags and the ≥K-items-at-top-tier rule (2 at an application top, 3 at a comprehension top). Questions carrying any of these show a flag in the review list below.

Instructor review & sign-off

Check off each question you've reviewed. Click a row to expand and view it; use “Edit in Editor →” to change its content. Review status is saved on this device and travels with an exported bank.

Config package builder

Build the config a colleague deploys to a class. Model 1 (default, for edited banks) exports one file (config + this bank) to hand off via your shared folder — students launch with ?cfg=<id>&sec=…. Model 2 makes a share link that carries the config in the URL and uses the stock bank (no edits). Use Schedule chapter releases below to drip-release chapters by date across the term; leave it off and every authored chapter is available at once.
